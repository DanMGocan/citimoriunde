These proposed periods require operator and legal review before launch. Retention variables are deployment configuration, not permission to retain everything. Cleanup must skip active loans, open disputes and legal holds, record why a hold is necessary, and schedule review. Neither suspended accounts nor deletion requests automatically return physical copies. A legal hold should constrain the minimum relevant records, with access restrictions and an accountable reviewer.

## Processing bases and rights procedure

For each processing purpose, the operator must record its actual lawful basis, necessity and recipients; assess legitimate interests where relied upon; separately request consent for optional processing; and publish the controller contact, applicable rights and supervisory-authority route. The DPC describes minimisation, limited retention, security and accountability as GDPR principles; this inventory helps record those decisions and does not prove compliance. [DPC principles](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/individuals/data-protection-basics/principles-data-protection).

1. Receive a correction, access/export or deletion request in account settings or through the public contact route. Record receipt, scope, responsible reviewer and due-date tracking appropriate to the applicable law. Verify control proportionately without routinely requesting an identity document.
2. Restrict the export to the requesting member's profile, preferences and relevant participant records. Redact counterparty contact information and unrelated private messages; omit internal moderation notes, access tokens and operational secrets. Participant messages require a privacy review to protect third-party rights.
3. Correct current profile data without rewriting historical actors, physical assertions or loan locations. Preserve original event data with a correction entry when necessary for a dispute.
4. On deletion, revoke sessions, remove public personal profile fields and unnecessary data, privately unlist owned copies, and review unresolved loans/report evidence. Document any retained subset, basis and expiry. A pseudonymised identifier remains personal data where relinking is possible.
5. Maintain a minimal deletion/restriction record needed to apply prior requests after restoration. Test that restored backups do not republish deleted data. Delete expired backup archives from local and offsite storage.

## Cookies and advertising review

Capture an actual browser storage/network inventory for signed-out, signed-in and refusing-consent visitors. Identify cookie name/provider/purpose/duration; include local storage and similar technology. Necessary session/security storage must not be stretched to cover advertising or analytics. DPC guidance identifies narrow exemptions and requires users to be able to vary or withdraw consent. [DPC cookie FAQ](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/faqs/cookies/my-website-or-app-uses-cookies-and-other-tracking-do-i-have-get-consent-users).

For UK targeting, reassess current PECR/UK GDPR rules and the latest ICO storage/access guidance, including changes following the Data (Use and Access) Act. The implementation conservatively blocks ad/analytics requests until required consent and after refusal; that choice does not establish that a CMP or notice is legally adequate. [ICO guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-storage-and-access-technologies/).

## Launch legal issues

- Determine EU intermediary/hosting/online-platform classification, the actual operator's establishment and EU contact/representation requirements, applicable notice/action, statement-of-reasons, complaint and transparency obligations. Size exemptions apply to some duties; they do not exempt an operator from all DSA obligations. Record the reasoned classification and review growth. [European Commission DSA questions](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/digital-services-act-questions-and-answers).
- Assess UK Online Safety Act scope for forum and messaging when serving UK users, even if hosted outside the UK. An adults-only declaration does not by itself dispose of children's access assessments or illegal-content obligations. Record scope, risk assessment, reporting/complaints and proportional measures with qualified review. [Ofcom service guide](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/guide-for-services), [current illegal-content duties](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/illegal-content-duties-under-the-online-safety-act?language=en).
- Have consumer terms reviewed for fairness, transparency, mandatory rights and the operator's own responsibilities. The platform coordination description must not become a blanket liability exclusion or forced venue/arbitration clause. [Irish CCPC unfair terms](https://www.ccpc.ie/consumer-advice/consumer-rights/buying-services/unfair-contract-terms).
- Obtain country-specific lending/copyright advice for the proposed member-to-member model; distinguish physical lending, permanent gifts and electronic copying. The EU Rental/Lending Directive's definitions and national implementation need review; a private network is not automatically a statutory public library. [Directive 2006/115/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32006L0115). UK guidance discusses libraries outside the statutory service, but does not resolve every country's rules or this exact model. [UK PLR guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/public-lending-right-how-it-applies).
- Review uploaded cover photographs and provider cover licences separately from ownership of a physical book. Record cover provenance and permission, provide takedown intake, and do not ingest copied book text or ebook/PDF files.
- Assess data-protection impact and transfer risks, actual security incidents/breach response, mandatory notices, accessibility obligations and competent authority contacts. Complete the operator's final legal review and publish approved Romanian document versions; generated drafts are not legal approval.
